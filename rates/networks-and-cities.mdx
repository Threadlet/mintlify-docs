---
title: "Supported networks, cities and states"
description: "The complete list of locations the business electricity rates API supports: all 13 National Electricity Market networks, 86 cities, 6 states and territories, and 2,221 postcodes."
---

The rates API covers every distribution network on Australia's National Electricity Market. This page lists **every** network and city slug it accepts.

| Location | Coverage | Parameter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Networks | All 13 on the National Electricity Market | `distributor` |
| Cities | 86 cities (all capitals except Perth and Darwin, plus regional cities) | `city` |
| States and territories | NSW, VIC, QLD, SA, TAS, ACT | `state` |
| Postcodes | 2,221 postcodes | `postcode` |
| Sites | Any NMI on the National Electricity Market | `nmi` |

<Info>
  **Town or suburb not listed?** It's still covered. Use its postcode, or the site's NMI, and the API finds the network that serves it.
</Info>

WA and NT aren't on the National Electricity Market, so locations there return `422 out_of_coverage`.
